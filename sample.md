## Key Takeaways

- **Most lethal**: Pancreatic cancer (5-year survival ~12%) and lung cancer (~25%) carry the worst prognoses due to late-stage diagnosis.
- **Most survivable**: Prostate cancer (~98% localized), breast cancer (~91%), and melanoma (~93% localized) when caught early.
- **Biomarkers drive management**: PSA (prostate), CA 125 (ovarian), CA 19-9 (pancreatic), CEA (colorectal) guide both diagnosis and treatment response monitoring.
- **Immunotherapy revolution**: Cancers like melanoma, lung, and lymphoma have seen dramatically improved outcomes with PD-1/PD-L1 and CTLA-4 checkpoint inhibitors.
- **HPV vaccination** is the most effective primary prevention strategy for cervical cancer, now also reducing oropharyngeal and anal cancer risk.
